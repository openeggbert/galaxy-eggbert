Galaxy Eggbert - third-party components
=======================================

Galaxy Eggbert itself is free software under the GNU General Public License,
version 3 (LICENSE).

Bundled with the program (Linux archive: lib/; Windows: next to the .exe;
Android: inside the APK):

- CNA, an XNA 4.0 reimplementation in C++ (Microsoft Public License, Ms-PL).
  https://github.com/libcna/cna
- cna-engine, helper modules for CNA (MIT License).
  https://github.com/libcna/cna-engine
- SDL3, SDL3_image and SDL3_mixer (zlib License). Their license texts are in
  licenses/SDL/ in the Linux archive.

Used from the system on Linux (not bundled): the graphics stack (OpenGL),
FFmpeg (libavcodec, libavformat, libavutil, libswresample; LGPL 2.1 or later,
or GPL depending on the distribution's build), the C and C++ runtimes.

Game data
---------

Galaxy Eggbert is a 3D remake of Speedy Blupi / Speedy Eggbert for mobile
(mobile-eggbert, MIT License, Copyright (c) 2013 Daniel Roux;
https://github.com/openeggbert/mobile-eggbert), from whose package its 2D
graphics, sounds and music come. Speedy Blupi and Speedy Eggbert are original
creations of EPSITEC SA. The 3D models and the 2x graphics were converted and
rendered from the original Ray Dream Studio scenes. The 3D worlds are original
Galaxy Eggbert designs.
