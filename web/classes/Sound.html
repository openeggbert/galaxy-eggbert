Sound

The 93 sound channels of Content/sounds/, played with the original's volume, pitch and conflict rules, heard from Blupi's place.

Headersrc/GalaxyEggbert/Game/Sound.hpp
Sourcesrc/GalaxyEggbert/Game/Sound.cpp
NamespaceGalaxyEggbert::Game
Channel enumGalaxyEggbert::Def::SoundChannel in include/GalaxyEggbert/Def/SoundChannel.hpp

Purpose

Sound owns one CNA SoundEffect and at most one SoundEffectInstance per channel. Channel n is Content/sounds/soundNNN.wav; a missing file makes that channel a silent no-op. Every play takes the channel's volume and pitch from kVolumePitch, ported with approval from Mobile Eggbert's tableVolumePitch, times the player's effects volume. A channel already playing is not restarted, except channel 10, as in the original. Music is not part of this class: the host plays it with CNA's MediaPlayer (Audio & Music).

Collaborators

  • Def::SoundChannel: the original's channel numbers 0-92 (0 reserved, never renumber).
  • CNA's Microsoft::Xna::Framework::Audio: SoundEffect, SoundEffectInstance, SoundState.
  • InteractionSystem receives a Sound& and plays the world's sounds (doors, pickups, enemies, voyages).
  • GalaxyEggbertGame owns sound_: loads it, sets the listener and the hidden flag every frame, plays Blupi's and the vehicles' sounds, applies the Setup's switch and volume.
  • BlupiController supplies the ground icon for PlayLand() and the motor state for the vehicle loops.
  • SaveData: sound on/off and effectsVolume= (0-100).

How it is used

Each frame of play, in src/GalaxyEggbertCNA/GalaxyEggbertGameUpdate.cpp:

sound_.SetBlupiHidden(phase_ == GalaxyEggbert::Def::GamePhase::Play && blupi_.IsHidden());
...
sound_.SetListener(blupi_.GetX(), blupi_.GetY(), blupi_.GetZ());

A world sound at a cell, here the lightning's zap:

sound_.PlayAt(GalaxyEggbert::Def::SoundChannel::SoundChannel69, cell.X, cell.Y, cell.Z);
  • A looped channel (a vehicle motor) is started with Play(channel, true) and ended with Stop(channel); the host's UpdateVehicleMotorSound() and StopVehicleMotorSound() do this for channels 15-18 and 28-31.
  • Inside the audible box a sound plays at full table volume; there is no distance fade and no stereo balance (GP-0412).
  • The pitch is applied as the original applies it (XNA octaves, clamped at 0 below); before 2026-11-02 it was missing (BD-0031).
  • An instance's looping flag is set only when the instance is created: XNA forbids changing it after the first play.

Tests

  • VerifyInteractionSystem (tools/VerifyInteractionSystem.cpp): MutedWhileHidden() for all 93 channels against the original's list, Audible() inside and at the edges, every range of FootstepChannelFor() and CeilingChannelFor(). The test links Sound without LoadContent(), so no audio device is used.
  • VerifyGESaveData: the effects and music volumes stored in savedata.txt.
