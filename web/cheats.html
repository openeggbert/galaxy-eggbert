Cheats & Diagnostics

The typed cheat words, the F12 cheat menu, the diagnostics they switch on, and how to build the game without them.

Mobile Eggbert has two ways to cheat: a hidden menu of nine cheats and words typed during play. Galaxy Eggbert keeps both, with the original's words and effects, and adds a few words of its own for diagnostics and fair play. As of 2026-11-10 the parser knows 33 words (help and cheats are the same one). The list is InputPad::TypedCheatWords() in src/GalaxyEggbert/Game/InputPad.cpp; the effects are applied in GalaxyEggbertGame::ApplyTypedCheat() and ApplyCheat() (src/GalaxyEggbertCNA/GalaxyEggbertGame.cpp).

Typing a cheat

  • Type the word during play, without Enter. Words are not read in menus, Pause or the editor.
  • Letters are read as ordered key presses through CNA's Keyboard::KeyDownEXT, so even very fast typing between two frames is kept, on every device, including keyboards used with touch screens. No IME or on-screen keyboard is needed. (This needs CNA 594eb28dc or newer.)
  • The game keeps the last 32 letters. A word fires when they end with it; if one word ends with another, the longer wins. The letters are cleared after a word, except after quick, so that quicklollypop can still be typed. Slow typing works too.
  • While the letters typed so far begin a word, those letter keys do not reach Blupi until released: the S that ends weelkeys does not turn him round.
  • Type help or cheats to pause the game under a panel listing every word with its explanation; F1, Esc or [Close] closes it. F1 alone shows the controls and works even when cheats are locked.

Diagnostics

debug

A panel with fixed label and value columns, so it does not jump as digits change: simulation time (minutes, seconds and 20 Hz ticks), mission and background region, lives, position and cell in raw 3D grid coordinates, velocity in blocks per second, heading, movement mode, animation and icon, game speed, the number of keys held and of touches, whether a gamepad is connected and whether accelerometer readings arrive. It sits at the upper right on a desktop and upper centre with touch controls, appears only in play (also in an editor play-test) and is drawn by src/GalaxyEggbert/Game/DebugOverlay.hpp.

compass

An aid in the lower right, off by default. It points to the nearest chest not yet taken, then, with every chest taken or none in the world, to the nearest red arrow or golden key. Three unlabelled rows: the straight-line distance in blocks, rounded; < or > for the shorter turn, | when he faces it (within 3 degrees); ^ or v when it is above or below, - at the same height (within 0.1 block). A direction, not a route finder. Touch layouts keep it clear of the controls.

position and fps

position lists x= y= z= in the editor's cell coordinates (x and z from the world's corner, y up) and draws three arrows under the list, X red, Y green, Z blue, turning with the camera, to show which way each axis runs. fps lists the frames drawn per second, measured every half second.

Developer diagnostics outside the game's cheats (environment variables, traces) are on Debugging.

fairplay and honest

fairplay turns off every switched-on cheat (megablupi, ghost, opendoors, showsecret, compass, debug, fps, position, quick and zoom; a speed above fast drops to normal) and locks cheat entry: no other word, no F12 and no hidden menu are taken. Typing fairplay again unlocks; the old toggles do not come back by themselves. Lives, bombs, ammunition, keys, vehicles, powers and world changes already gained stay. honest does the same and keeps the lock until the game quits: not even fairplay or honest is taken any more. Both locks are kept in the resume file, so a resumed mission has them again.

xnjttjpo: edit the world being played

Speedy Blupi's XMISSION cheat, typed with every letter after the first moved one on (x-n-j-t-t-j-p-o), switched its editor to the official missions. Here it opens the 3D world editor on the world being played (not during a demo, a recording or an editor play-test). Saving writes the shipped world file itself and keeps the version before it in customworlds/campaign-backups/; Stop returns to the mission from its start. An edited world shows Original in Pause, which brings back the world as shipped. On the web the edit is kept in the browser's storage and laid over the packed worlds at every start.

The F12 cheat menu

In play, F12 opens and closes the original's hidden cheat menu: a row of nine square buttons across the top of the screen, a letter under each. Clicking or tapping one closes the menu and applies the cheat.

ButtonLetterCheat
1DDoors: as opendoors, without the keys
2BSuperBlupi: as megablupi
3SAs showsecret
4EAs layegg: nine lives
5RReset: erases the selected player's progress, as the Setup's Erase progress
6TTrial: no effect (the original's trial mode cannot be reached)
7CAs cleanall
8TAs alltreasure
9GAs endgoal
Button R is not undoable. It erases the current player's saved progress at once.

The original's way in also works during play: ten taps or clicks on six invisible zones covering the upper-left two thirds of the width and 57 % of the height, three columns by two rows. In (column, row) order: (1,2) (2,2) (3,2) (1,2) (1,1) (2,1) (2,2) (2,1) (3,1) (3,2). A wrong zone starts the count again.

Building without cheats

The CMake option GALAXY_EGGBERT_ENABLE_CHEATS (default ON) sets the macro of the same name, read in src/GalaxyEggbert/Game/CheatConfig.hpp as kCheatsEnabled:

cmake -S . -B build-cna-opengl3 -DGALAXY_EGGBERT_ENABLE_CHEATS=OFF
cmake --build build-cna-opengl3 --target GalaxyEggbertCNA

With it OFF every typed word (help, compass, debug and xnjttjpo included), F12, the hidden menu and the application of cheats are compiled out; the F1 panel says "Cheats disabled" and no longer lists F12 and F7/F8. fairplay cannot turn them back on. Both configurations were built and tested; set the option back to ON and rebuild to restore them. VerifyGEInputPad tests the parser and the locks.

Cheats, demos and resume

A main-menu demo freezes the player's cheats with the rest of the profile and gives them back after it. The resume file records the typed letters, the compass, debug and the locks, so an interrupted mission resumes with the same cheats. See Demos & Recordings and Save Data & Resume.
